# Client Guide

A walkthrough for participants using their CourtBridge Solutions portal.

## ⚠ Your record is permanent
Once you check in or send a message, it is timestamped and saved — it cannot be edited or deleted by you or anyone else. This is by design: your compliance record has to be trustworthy in front of a judge, probation officer, or program provider. If you make a mistake, do not try to "fix" it yourself — contact your provider directly through Messages.

## Checking in
Tap **Check In Now** on your home screen. Your location and the time are captured automatically — there is nothing to fill out. This only happens the moment you tap the button; the app does not track your location in the background. Your streak counter updates right away. If you've missed a check-in, you'll see a gentle "let's get back on track" message instead of nothing — no shame, just a nudge.

No signal or no internet where you are? Check in anyway — it saves right on your phone and sends itself in automatically the moment you're back online. Your streak and record are safe either way.

## Your Dashboard (Home)
Shows your check-in streak, a daily affirmation, your compliance rate, and whatever is coming up next — a task or court date — front and center. If a form needs your signature, a "Needs your attention" banner appears here too, linking straight to it.

## My Journey
See your actual court orders/programs (you may have more than one running at once), each with a progress bar showing how far along you are. Achievement badges are awarded automatically for check-in streaks (7/30/90/180/365 days) and when you complete a program. Progress Notes from your provider show up here too — just a date at first; tap one to open a printable record you can save as a PDF.

## My Documents
Any documents your provider has shared with you (court orders, intake forms, etc.) — tap to view.

## Forms to Sign
Any forms your provider needs you to sign electronically (waivers, releases, etc.) show up here. Read the form, type your name to sign, and it's saved permanently. Once signed, tap it anytime under "Signed Forms" to view or save a PDF copy.

## Messages
Go to the **Messages** tab to reach your provider directly. If something is urgent, check **"Mark urgent"** before sending — this notifies your provider right away instead of waiting for them to check messages.

## Court Dates
Go to the **Court Dates** tab to see everything scheduled for you.

## My Progress
Shows what week you're on in your program and what's due before your next scheduled visit — plus a short note on how your data is protected (see below).

## Your Privacy & Security
Your records are private — only you and your provider can see them. CourtBridge Solutions does not sell or share your information. Questions or problems should go to **your provider**, not CourtBridge Solutions directly.

## If your status changes
If your provider marks your account inactive or terminated, you'll see a simple message instead of the full app — nothing is deleted, and your provider can reactivate you (just a quick address/phone check, not starting over) whenever you're ready to continue.

## If you're in a CATCH Court program
If you are ever unsafe, go to Messages and check "Mark urgent" before sending. Your provider is notified immediately, including by text.
