# Legal & Compliance Notes
DRAFT, needs an actual attorney's review before being treated as authoritative

- CourtBridge is a record-keeping and communication tool. It does not provide legal advice, and nothing in the app should be presented to a client, court, or agency as such.
- SMS/text messaging to clients requires documented prior consent (see the consent checkbox added to Client Intake / enrollment on 2026-07-21) — this exists because of TCPA exposure, and the current consent language is a placeholder that has NOT been reviewed by a lawyer.
- Client data (check-ins, GPS location, drug test results, CPS/PO/violation records) is sensitive and may be subject to state-specific privacy or court-records handling rules depending on jurisdiction and program type — not yet researched or documented here.

Treat this whole document as a "things to get a lawyer to look at" list, not as settled legal guidance.
- Program Waiver & Consent checkbox added to Client Intake / enrollment on 2026-07-22 (required to complete signup, sets clients.terms_signed_at) — same status as the SMS consent language: a placeholder that has NOT been reviewed by a lawyer. Also flagged: providers may want additional signable forms/waivers beyond this one (discussed but not yet built).
