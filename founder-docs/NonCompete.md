# Non-Compete / Confidentiality
DO NOT USE AS-IS

This is empty on purpose. Non-compete agreements are legally binding, vary heavily by state (some states, and a 2024 FTC rule, restrict or ban them outright for many workers), and getting this wrong can mean the agreement is unenforceable or even creates liability for the business.

Do not draft this yourself or have an AI draft binding language for it — have an employment attorney in your state write this one specifically before it's used with anyone.
