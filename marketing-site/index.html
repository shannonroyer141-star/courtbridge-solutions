The Problem

Are you confident your current process
would withstand an audit today?

Most compliance tracking isn't really a system — it's spreadsheets, paper files, and frantic effort when reports are due. With requirements on the rise, that approach is no longer sustainable.

01

No Central Record

Check-ins, appointments, and program milestones scattered across email, paper, and memory. When courts ask for proof — it's a scramble.

02

Missed Alerts

A client misses a check-in. Nobody finds out until the next session — or the next court date. Early intervention becomes impossible.

03

Reporting Gaps

Providers spend hours assembling compliance reports by hand. Inconsistencies create risk for clients and programs alike — and affect funding.

The Platform

Everything your program needs.
Nothing you don't.

Built for boutique specialty court programs — drug courts, DUI courts, mental health courts, domestic violence intervention programs, and more.

01 — GPS Verification

GPS Check-In Verification

Clients check in from their phone with real-time GPS capture. Timestamp and location logged automatically — no manual entry required.

02 — Alerts

Missed Check-In Alerts

See who's missed a check-in the moment you open your dashboard — flagged against their own schedule, not a generic daily rule. Catch issues early before they become compliance violations.

03 — Enrollment

Client Enrollment by Link

Send a secure enrollment link via SMS or email. Clients complete onboarding from their phone in minutes and land in InterventionConnect™ — their own portal, from their provider. No app store required.

04 — Dashboard

Compliance Dashboards

Real-time view of every client's status — check-ins, phases, upcoming appointments, and flags. Know your caseload at a glance.

05 — Reporting

Court-Ready Reports

Generate clean, consistent compliance reports on demand. Hand off to courts with confidence — documentation that stands up to scrutiny.

06 — Access

Role-Based Access

Separate portals for providers and clients. Zero-PHI architecture — everyone sees exactly what they need, nothing more.

07 — Messaging

Two-Way Messaging

Providers and clients message directly in-app. A client can flag a message urgent — it pings the provider's phone immediately, no waiting for the next check-in or court date.

08 — Progress

Client Journey & Achievements

Court-order progress tracking with milestones clients actually see and earn — not just a record kept on them. Completions auto-generate a real certificate.

09 — Collaboration

Justice Collaboration Exchange

A cross-agency directory and resource marketplace, plus a request-and-approve model for records disclosure — courts and other agencies request access, your org decides, every decision logged.

Our Story

Built From the Inside Out.

"Providers should be caregivers, not administrators."

CourtBridge Solutions wasn't built in a conference room. I've been on both sides of the specialty court system myself — as someone who went through it, and later as a service provider working inside one. I've seen this world from both directions.

The tools that exist today were built for enterprise agencies with enterprise budgets. The boutique programs — the ones doing the hardest, most personal work — were left to improvise. CourtBridge Solutions is built for them.

In Phase 1, our focus is on simple, structured compliance tracking without the storage of clinical or treatment data. As we move forward, CourtBridge Solutions is evolving into a more advanced platform to better support providers as their needs grow.

Shannon RoyerFounder & CEO, CourtBridge Solutions LLC · Lakewood Ranch, FL
