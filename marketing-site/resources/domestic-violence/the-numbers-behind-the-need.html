Domestic Violence Data Series

The Numbers Behind the Need

CourtBridge Solutions

More than 1 in 3 women and 1 in 6 men have experienced contact sexual violence, physical violence, or stalking by an intimate partner during their lifetime.

More than 11,000 people were murdered in domestic-violence cases between 2020 and 2024 — more than six lives lost every day.

Behind every number is a person, a family, a provider, and a community affected by violence.

These statistics reinforce why effective intervention matters — and why the systems surrounding that work must support reliable documentation, meaningful accountability, and stronger connections.

Providers perform the intervention. CourtBridge Solutions is building technology to strengthen the infrastructure surrounding that work — verified, connected, and accountable.
